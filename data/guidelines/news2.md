[NEWS2-3.1]
An aggregate NEWS2 score of 0-4 indicates low clinical risk and routine
ward-based monitoring, with escalation only if a single parameter scores 3.

[NEWS2-3.2]
An aggregate NEWS2 score of 5-6, or any single parameter scored 3, is
medium clinical risk and warrants an urgent review by a clinician
competent to assess acutely ill patients.

[NEWS2-3.3]
An aggregate NEWS2 score of 7 or more is high clinical risk and requires
emergency assessment, usually by a team with critical care competencies.

[NEWS2-4.1]
Patients with known hypercapnic respiratory failure (e.g. severe COPD)
should have their oxygen saturation scored on Scale 2 with an individual
target range agreed by a senior clinician, since a normal Scale 1 SpO2
target can be inappropriate for these patients.

[NEWS2-4.2]
Beta-blockers and other rate-controlling medications can blunt the
tachycardic response to physiological stress, so a heart-rate rise in a
patient on these medications may under-represent the severity of an
underlying deterioration.

[SEPSIS-1.1]
In a patient with a suspected infection, a coordinated rise in heart rate
and respiratory rate together with a fall in blood pressure and oxygen
saturation should raise concern for sepsis and prompt urgent clinical
review rather than being treated as isolated findings.
